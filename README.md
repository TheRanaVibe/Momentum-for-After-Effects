**Momentum is a professional secondary motion workflow system for Adobe After Effects, designed to bridge the gap between creative ideation and performance-optimized final output.**

It provides a seamless, intuitive, and non-destructive workflow to add physically-believable bounce, overshoot, squash, and stretch to your animations, empowering you to focus on the idea, while Momentum handles the physics.

## Core Features

*   **✨ Intuitive 3-Step Workflow:** A simple UI guides you through the entire process: **1. Animate** with keyframes, **2. Enhance** with physics, and **3. Bake** for final performance.
*   **💪 Non-Destructive Rigs:** Physics are applied via live expression rigs. Tweak, change, or remove them at any time without destroying your original keyframes.
*   **🚀 Real-Time Feedback:** Tweak physics parameters like "Elasticity" or "Stiffness" directly from the Effect Controls panel and see your animation update instantly in the composition.
*   **🔥 Unified Baking Engine:** When you're done, finalize your work with a single click. The powerful baker intelligently converts all selected Momentum rigs (Bounce and S&S) into clean keyframes, giving you maximum performance for rendering.
*   **💾 Save & Share Presets:** Perfected a specific feel? Save your custom-tweaked Bounce or Squash & Stretch settings as a User Preset to instantly reuse in any project.
*   **❤️ Free & For the Community:** Momentum is completely free to remove all barriers to entry for students, freelancers, and studios alike.

This tool is, and will always be, **free**. It was created to give back to the motion design community.

### Key Features

*   **Non-Destructive Rigs:** Apply interactive physics rigs as simple effect controllers, allowing you to tweak and art-direct your secondary motion in real-time.
*   **Two Physics Engines:**
    *   **Bounce:** A powerful and versatile engine for creating realistic bounce and overshoot on any keyframed property (Position, Scale, Rotation).
    *   **Squash & Stretch (S&S):** Automatically adds character and life to your layers with velocity-based stretching and impact-based jiggles.
*   **Additive Animation Presets:** Kickstart your motion with a set of fully-customizable, built-in animation presets that apply keyframes additively to your layer's current state.
*   **Unified Bake Engine:** When your scene gets heavy, finalize your work with a single click. The intelligent bake engine converts all selected Momentum rigs into clean, optimized keyframes, restoring real-time playback.
*   **User Preset System:** Love a specific feel? Save your custom-tweaked Bounce or S&S rig settings as a user preset for instant recall in future projects.
*   **Professional Workflow:** From a "Rotating Body Mode" for S&S to a "Bake to Duplicate" feature that preserves your original rigs, every feature is built with the professional in mind.

## Installation

1.  Go to the **[Releases page](https://github.com/YourUsername/YourRepoName/releases)** on the right-hand side.
2.  Download the `Momentum.jsx` file from the latest release.
3.  In After Effects, go to `File > Scripts > Run Script File...` and select the `Momentum.jsx` file.

**For permanent installation as a dockable panel:**
*   Place the `Momentum.jsx` file into the `ScriptUI Panels` folder for your After Effects installation.
*   Restart After Effects.
*   You can then open it from the `Window` menu at the top.

## Known Limitations

*   **Time-Remapped Layers:** Physics results may be unpredictable on footage layers that have time-remapping enabled. It is recommended to pre-compose time-remapped layers *before* applying Momentum.
*   **Complex Parenting:** Using the S&S "Rotating Body Mode" on a layer that is already part of a complex parent-child chain may lead to unexpected transformations. Please test carefully in these specific situations.
